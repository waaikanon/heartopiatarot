# ✨ Heartopia Tarot Companion ✨

Welcome to the **Heartopia Tarot Companion**! This is a web-based utility tool designed to assist players with the fortune-telling table (Tarot reading) mechanics in the game **Heartopia**. 

Whether you are looking for card meanings, spread layouts, or a summary of your readings, this companion app makes your in-game fortune-telling experience smoother and more immersive.

## ✨ Features
* **In-Game Assistance:** Specifically crafted to complement the tarot/fortune-telling features in Heartopia.
* **Bilingual Support:** Fully supports both **English** and **Thai** languages.
* **Multiple Spread Layouts:** Choose from various card spreads such as:
  * Spark of Inspiration (1 Card)
  * Stream of Time (3 Cards)
  * Clearing Doubts (3 Cards)
  * Identity Jigsaw (4 Cards)
  * Crossroads (5 Cards)
  * World of Relationships (6 Cards)
* **Detailed Interpretations:** Provides keywords, in-depth meanings, and advice for both Upright and Reversed cards (Major & Minor Arcana).
* **Interactive UI:** A beautiful, responsive glass-morphism design with soothing audio feedback (soft clicks and chimes) for a magical experience.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Open the website on your browser (PC or Mobile).
2. Select your preferred language (TH/EN) at the top right.
3. Choose a question or topic you want to ask.
4. Select the card spread layout that matches your in-game reading.
5. Click on the empty slots to draw your cards (you can also toggle them Upright/Reversed).
6. Click **"✨ Generate Reading ✨"** to get a full summary and advice based on your drawn cards!
